FAQ

Questions? We've Got You.

Here are answers to some of the most common questions.

Do I need to be fit before I start?

Not at all. You can start exactly where you are. Your workouts will be adjusted to your current fitness level and progressed as you get stronger.

What if I've never trained before?

That's completely fine. You'll be shown what to do, how to do the exercises, and how to train safely. You don't need to know anything before your first session.

Can personal training help me lose weight?

It can help, but training is only one part of weight loss. We'll help you become more active, build strength, and create a routine you can stick to. Your nutrition and consistency matter too.

What happens during a session?

You'll work through a structured workout with your trainer. You'll be coached through each exercise, and the workout will be adjusted to your ability and goals.
